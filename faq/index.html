Frequently Asked Questions

Fill out the contact form or send me an email. Once we align on details, I send a contract and invoice for a 25% retainer to hold your date. Your date isn't reserved until both are signed and received.

A 25% retainer is due at booking. The remaining balance is due 2 weeks before your session or event date. I accept credit/debit cards and bank transfer, processed securely through Found. Sales tax is added to each invoice where applicable.

Portrait, family, and maternity sessions: approximately 4 weeks. Weddings: approximately 8 weeks. Commercial projects: on a timeline we agree on when we plan the project. Rush delivery may be available — just ask when you inquire.

Your private gallery stays online for 90 days after delivery, which gives you plenty of time to download your photos.

Yes — I'm available to travel anywhere. Travel within 50 miles of Tri-Cities is always included. Beyond that, travel costs are added to the package at cost (mileage, lodging if required, flights for destination sessions).

Life happens, and I'll work with you. For portrait, family, and commercial sessions, requests made more than 2 weeks before your session are accommodated at no charge, based on my availability. Weddings are harder to move because dates book far in advance, so rescheduling a wedding depends on my availability for your new date. Please refer to your contract for full details.
