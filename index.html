About

Alexandra Lynn, photographer
Hey, I'm Alexandra.

Photographer. Storyteller.
Eastern Washington local.

I'm a photographer based in Tri-Cities, WA — and I genuinely love what I do. I'm drawn to the real moments: the nervous laugh before a first look, the way a family collapses into each other during a golden-hour session, the confidence someone feels when they see themselves in a photograph that actually looks like them.

My style is warm, romantic, and unposed. I don't believe in stiff poses or manufactured smiles — I believe in chasing good light, building real connection, and letting the moment lead.

I'm based in Kennewick and serve all of Tri-Cities, including Kennewick, Richland, and Pasco, and I'm available to travel anywhere. The Columbia River Gorge, the Olympic Peninsula, a destination wedding in Italy — I'm always up for an adventure.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Photos by Alexandra Lynn ("I," "me," "my") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information I collect through this website (photosbyalexandralynn.com), how I use it, and the choices you have.

Information I Collect

When you fill out a contact form on this site, I collect the information you provide: your first and last name, email address, phone number (if you choose to share it), the type of service you're interested in, your approximate date or timeframe, and any message or details you include. I don't collect any other personal information through this website, and I don't use cookies or analytics tools to track your visit.

How I Use Your Information

I use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss your session, and, if we move forward, to plan and deliver your photography services. I never sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How Your Information Is Handled

Contact form submissions on this site are processed through Netlify Forms, the hosting platform this website runs on. Netlify stores submitted form data securely and forwards new submissions to my email inbox. You can read more about how Netlify handles data in their own privacy policy at netlify.com.

Client Galleries

Once you're a client, your photo gallery is delivered through Pixieset, a separate third-party gallery platform. Pixieset's collection and use of your information for gallery hosting and delivery is governed by Pixieset's own privacy policy, not this one.

Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. The site does load fonts from Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to request font files from Google's servers (including your IP address) in order to display them — this is standard for most websites and is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Data Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through this site, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and I can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children through the site's contact forms. Photographs taken of minors during family or maternity sessions are handled separately, under the terms of your client contract and any model release you sign — not through this website.

Your Choices

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete the information you've submitted through this site's forms at any time by emailing me at photosbyalexandralynn@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
