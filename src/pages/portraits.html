FAQ

Fill out the contact form or send me an email. Once we align on details, I send a contract and invoice for a 25% retainer to hold your date. Your date isn't reserved until both are signed and received.

A 25% retainer is due at booking. The remaining balance is due 2 weeks before your session or event date. I accept credit/debit cards and bank transfer, processed securely through Pixieset. Sales tax is added to each invoice where applicable.

The retainer reserves your date, so it is non-refundable once paid. If I'm ever unable to photograph your session and can't offer a replacement or a new date, you'll be refunded what you've paid for that session. Cancellation terms depend on your session type and timing, and are set out in your contract.

Portrait, family, and maternity sessions: approximately 4 weeks. Weddings: approximately 8 weeks. Rush delivery may be available — just ask when you inquire.
